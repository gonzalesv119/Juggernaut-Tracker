How the tracker marks progress

Green + crossed out: you met every rep target in that prescription block.
Blue PR: an AMRAP produced a higher estimated 1RM than another AMRAP you have logged for that lift.
Baseline: the first AMRAP logged for a lift. It becomes the number future AMRAPs can beat.
Accessories: each workout has five. Names and prescriptions are editable, so you can swap movements whenever you want.
AMRAP note: the “+” set still follows the phase guidance from your chart: Week 1 leave 2–3 reps, Week 2 leave 1–2 reps, Week 3 leave 0 reps, Week 4 has no + set.
